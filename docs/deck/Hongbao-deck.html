How it works

Three steps, all on Mezo.

01 · FUND

Fill the packet

MUSD with a permit signature, no separate approval; BTC swapped on Mezo’s Tigris pool; or MUSD from your Mezo loan.

02 · WAIT

Earn while it waits

The packet sits as sMUSD in Mezo’s MUSD Savings Rate vault until friends claim.

03 · CLAIM

Land in Mezo

The link signs a claim, a relayer submits it, and the share lands in the friend’s own name, staked by default, plus BTC for gas.

VaultGauge.deposit(amount, recipient)
Nothing gets stuck. The sender can refund any time; after expiry, anyone can send the rest back to the sender.
The key stays in the link. It lives in the #fragment, never on a server, and each claim is signed for one recipient and payout.

Business model

Branded onboarding packets for Mezo projects.

A project funds the packets; its community lands in Mezo savings, staked by default.

1%

Packet fee

Paid in MUSD when a packet is created.

Float

Savings yield

Unclaimed packets earn the MUSD savings rate for the treasury.

Tips

Self-paying relays

Each share carries a tiny BTC tip that pays whoever relays the claim. No gas subsidy.

PER FRIEND

Landing one friend in Mezo savings costs 1% of their share plus a 0.000004 BTC gas kit by default: 0.000003 for their own gas, 0.000001 for the relay.

Safety

No admin key, not even ours, can move the money.

  • No owner, no upgrade path, no admin withdrawal.
  • A guardian can pause new packets and set caps within hard limits fixed at deployment (mainnet: 100 / 500 / 5,000 MUSD).
  • Senders refund any time; anyone can return an expired packet to its sender.
  • Each claim is signed for one recipient, payout and relayer.
