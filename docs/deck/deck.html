Safety

Nobody, including us, can move the money.

  • No owner, no upgrade path. A guardian can only pause new packets and lower caps.
  • Sender can refund any time; anyone can return expired packets to the sender.
  • If the gauge or the vault refuses, recipients and senders are paid in kind.
  • Mainnet capped beta: 50 MUSD per packet, 500 outstanding.

Tested and reviewed
